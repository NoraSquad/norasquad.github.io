What you get

A character pack made to be used straight away, with clean naming and nothing you need to fix first.

Ready-to-use prefabs

Every character is a prefab with an Animator and the NoraCharacter component: PlayIdle, PlayMove, PlayAttack, PlaySkill.

2–4 animations each

Idle, walk or run, attack and a skill on heroes. In-place motion, uniquely named takes (NS_Knight_Attack).

Impact events

onHit and onSkill fire on the impact frame, ready for damage, sounds or your own effects.

Unlit or Lit

The same 2048² hand-painted textures, shown exactly as painted or reacting to your scene lights. Switch at runtime.

Mobile friendly

4.8k–15.8k triangles per character, 1024² ASTC texture override on Android and iOS, SRP Batcher compatible.

Demo scenes

A Showcase and a Lineup scene, plus optional demo effects you can switch off or remove in one click.
